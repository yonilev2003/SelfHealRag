Think about the scale
One employee gets a wrong stipend number — an annoyance.

Ten thousand employees get it — that's an operating-system problem for your company's own knowledge.
The lesson
The first time I ran this frozen test, SelfHeal tied the baseline — 0/3, on the exact case it was built for. Not a crash. Just a quiet, forgettable number.

The bug: memory only learned from training data, never from what it was actually asked, live. A good test doesn't just measure whether you generalize — it catches you lying to yourself about scope, silently.
A RAG system that retrieves information is useful.
One that knows when its own knowledge has expired
is something I'd actually trust inside a company.
