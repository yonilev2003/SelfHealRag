## VPN Access Policy

All employees must use the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.

<!-- chunk_id: it-vpn-v1-c02 entity_key: it.vpn_session_timeout_hours effective_date: 2026-01-10 supersedes_id: null -->
VPN sessions automatically time out after 8 hours of inactivity and require re-authentication via SSO.
