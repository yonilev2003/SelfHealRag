## Customer Refund Policy

Customer service representatives may approve refunds up to $500 without manager sign-off.

<!-- chunk_id: finance-refund-v1-c02 entity_key: finance.refund_cap_usd effective_date: 2026-01-05 supersedes_id: null -->
Refunds above this amount require a finance manager's written approval before processing.
