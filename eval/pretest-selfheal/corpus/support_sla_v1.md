## Support SLA Policy

All customer support tickets are triaged by the on-duty support engineer within one business day.

<!-- chunk_id: support-sla-v1-c02 entity_key: support.sla_response_hours effective_date: 2026-01-20 supersedes_id: null -->
Tickets must receive a first response from a support engineer within 24 hours of submission.
