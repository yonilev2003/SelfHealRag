## Customer Data Retention

Customer account data, including order history and support tickets, is subject to a defined retention period after account closure.

<!-- chunk_id: compliance-retention-v1-c02 entity_key: compliance.data_retention_months effective_date: 2026-02-01 supersedes_id: null -->
Data is retained for 24 months after account closure, after which it is permanently deleted from production systems.
