## Customer Refund Policy

Customer service representatives may approve refunds up to $750 without manager sign-off.

<!-- chunk_id: finance-refund-v2-c02 entity_key: finance.refund_cap_usd effective_date: 2026-06-15 supersedes_id: finance-refund-v1-c01 -->
Refunds above this amount require a finance manager's written approval before processing.
